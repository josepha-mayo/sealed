# Sealed — a result a contract can use

Private exams. Public scores. Markets that settle themselves.

## The opening

An artifact claimed a perfect score. MPC scored it one out of sixty-four.
Which number should move the money? Sealed makes the committed exam and
the computed result the settlement source.

## Who this is for

Start with an evaluation sponsor choosing a model for one measurable task.
They fund a threshold bounty, disclose the necessary questions to runners,
and pay the first qualifying result. This is a commercial hypothesis to test,
not a claim of customer demand. Markets can compose on the same result account.

## The demo

Open the case file. Inspect the saved mismatch. Replay the receipt-cloning
attack: each receipt is genuine, but a duplicate cannot buy extra weight.
Decrypt the authorized viewer's questions while answer fingerprints stay in
MPC. Run the complete replay. Keep the provenance visible.

## Why this uses crypto

Arcium jointly computes against encrypted state. Solana fixes the output
commitment and exposes the result account to escrow and settlement contracts.
A hosted evaluator can keep a secret exam, but the sponsor still trusts its
score and payout operator. Sealed splits that result path under the MPC
committee's assumptions and makes the evidence portable.

## What the prototype proves

Custody, scoring and settlement integrity for the shipped task families.
The arithmetic demo is not a frontier intelligence test. The saved replay is
a merged localnet capture; a devnet memo is a timestamp, not proof of a fresh
MPC run. Runner-declared model names are not verified model identity.

## Next milestone

Validate repeat sponsored evaluations with independent users: task relevance,
setup time, result latency, repeat usage and willingness to pay. Add runner
attestation and harder confidential tasks after validating the sponsor need.

## Event fit

Target the Solana ecosystem track. Functionality comes from replayable
scoring and payouts; novelty comes from confidential resolution state; UX
comes from a judge who can inspect, attack and decrypt without setup;
open-source composability comes from Run.correct and portable receipts.
The business plan is a specific sponsor pilot, with demand still unverified.
